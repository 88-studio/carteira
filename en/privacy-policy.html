Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

Carteira - Budget Planner ("the App") is developed by Henrique Horbovyi. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data You Provide

When you use the App, you may enter the following information, which is stored locally on your device only:

  • Budgets and budget allocations
  • Expense details (amount, description, category, date, recurrence)
  • Buckets (name, allocation, color, category)
  • Financial goals and goal records
  • Fixed expenses
  • Custom categories
  • Feedback form data (name, email, message)

1.2 Notification Data (Expense Tracker)

If you enable the optional Expense Tracker feature, the App uses Android's Notification Listener Service to read notifications from banking and financial apps you select. The following data is read:

  • Notification title and text content
  • Extended notification text
  • The package name of the originating app

This data is processed on your device to detect expense amounts and is never transmitted to any server. You control which apps are monitored and can disable this feature at any time.

1.3 Automatically Collected Data

The App uses third-party services that may collect certain information automatically:

Service Data Collected Purpose
Firebase Analytics Anonymous usage events, device type, OS version, app version Understand how the App is used and improve the experience
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, stack traces, device model, OS version Identify and fix bugs and crashes
Google AdMob Advertising ID, device information, IP address, ad interaction data Display ads to support free app features

1.4 Analytics Events

The App tracks the following anonymous events through Firebase Analytics:

  • First app open
  • Onboarding completion
  • Notification permission granted or denied
  • Manual expense added
  • Expense added via notification
  • Bucket created

These events do not contain personal financial data such as amounts, descriptions, or account information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Local data: Used exclusively within the App to provide budget planning, expense tracking, and financial goal features.
  • Analytics data: Used to understand usage patterns and improve the App.
  • Crash data: Used to identify and resolve technical issues.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • All personal financial data (expenses, budgets, goals, categories) is stored locally on your device using an encrypted local database and secure preferences.
  • No financial data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Analytics and crash data are transmitted to Google's Firebase services using encrypted connections.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and general usage data to serve and personalize ads, in accordance with Google's advertising privacy policy.

Important: Financial data read from notifications through the Expense Tracker feature — including transaction amounts, merchant names, and any other notification content — is never shared with Google AdMob or any other ad provider. This data is processed entirely on your device and is not used for ad targeting or personalization in any way.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal financial data with third parties. The data shared externally is limited to:

  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports sent to Firebase.
  • Device identifiers and ad interaction data shared with Google AdMob for serving advertisements. No financial data, notification content, or expense information is ever shared with ad providers.

6. Permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

Permission Reason
Internet Required for Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, and serving ads via Google AdMob
Post Notifications To send local reminders and alerts
Notification Listener Optional: to read banking notifications for automatic expense tracking

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Your Rights

Since your financial data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it:

  • Delete all data: Clear the App's data through your device settings or uninstall the App.
  • Disable Expense Tracker: Revoke notification access at any time through the App's settings or your device's system settings.
  • Opt out of analytics: You can limit ad tracking and analytics through your device settings.

9. Third-Party Links and Services

The App may contain links to third-party services (e.g., app store for ratings). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
